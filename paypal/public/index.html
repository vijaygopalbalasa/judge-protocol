What these checks guarantee

    The payment contract

    1. 1
      Agree the checks

      AI turns a plain request into objective checks. The buyer can edit them.

    2. 2
      Hold, do not charge

      The buyer approves a PayPal authorization tied to the exact checks hash.

    3. 3
      Deliver and verify

      The worker opens a private job link. Only the agreed program judges the bytes.

    4. 4
      Settle by the result

      Pass: capture and payout. Fail or timeout: void the hold. Every step leaves a receipt.

    Scope: repeatable checks can verify form, required content and JSON structure. They cannot prove truth, taste or accuracy.

    Judge Pay is a sandbox prototype built on Judge Protocol. No custody of buyer funds beyond PayPal's authorization hold.
