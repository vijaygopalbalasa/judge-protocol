    How the money moves

    1. Agree the checks

      AI turns a plain request into objective checks. You can edit every one.

    2. Hold, do not charge

      You approve a PayPal authorization tied to the exact checks.

    3. Deliver

      The worker, a person or the AI worker, delivers through a private job link.

    4. Settle by the result

      The checks run on the delivered file. Pass: PayPal captures and pays the worker. Fail or no delivery by the deadline: the hold is voided.

    Checks can verify structure, exact values, required content and length. They cannot judge truth or taste, so Judge Pay is for work a program can check.
